MISSION

Correct ResearchOne so that it reliably produces the research deliverable the user confirmed, supports genuinely deep and auditable research across a wide range of research types, minimizes bias and misinformation through an always-on evidence-integrity core, and applies the unique PolicyOne methodology only to the subset of runs for which it is selected or appropriate.

This is a runtime-correctness task, not a copy-editing exercise and not a request to add more prompts to an already prompt-heavy pipeline. The current failure is architectural: a correct ResearchBrief is created, but the execution planner, generic section generator, non-blocking validators, and universal PolicyOne prompt treatment override or ignore it.

ResearchOne must not equate rigor with adversarial treatment of every topic. Ordinary research should answer the question from the best available sources with calibrated uncertainty. Bias-aware synthesis should broaden and balance the evidence base. Claim verification should adjudicate a disputed claim. PolicyOne should be a distinct investigative option. These are different jobs.

The additional assessment correctly identified four useful facts:

Three recommendations require modification rather than direct adoption:

- Do not implement a blanket "PolicyOne bypass." Separate the always-on evidence-integrity core from optional premise adjudication and PolicyOne analysis. Otherwise the bypass could also suppress legitimate misinformation detection.

The assessment's "epistemic hijacking" label describes the symptom. The deeper defect is cross-axis contamination: research intent, premise treatment, depth, analytical methodology, source strategy, writer style, and specialist selection are being collapsed into one generic epistemic workflow.

3.8 Source strategy is planned but not enforced

Files:

- backend/src/services/discovery/discoveryOrchestrator.ts
- backend/src/services/reasoning/researchOrchestrator.ts
- backend/src/config/index.ts

The confirmed plan expected 25-45 sources. Runtime defaults cap discovery ingestion at 10. Retrieval executes at most five queries, each with a fixed top-K, and then proceeds regardless of source count, source independence, coverage, or remaining gaps.

The system retrieved 63 chunks from only 6 sources. Chunk volume was mistaken for source breadth.

Required correction:

- Thread sourceRequirements and depth into discovery.

- Replace the fixed two-round search with a bounded coverage loop.

- Enforce the confirmed minimum usable-source requirement or mark the report degraded before drafting.
- Permit depth-specific budgets rather than a universal cap of 10.

Recommended defaults, adjustable by topic:

- quick: 3-8 usable sources;
- standard: 8-15 usable sources;
- deep: 20-40 usable sources;
- exhaustive: 40-80+ usable sources or a documented saturation stop.

These are budgets, not proof of quality. A primary-source-heavy question may need fewer; a market landscape may need more. The confirmed plan should state the actual criteria.

3.9 Discovery ranking lacks coverage and diversity controls

Implement a Source Selection Matrix that scores:

- recency;

Use quota-aware selection so ten highly ranked pages from one vendor cannot crowd out primary documents, customer complaints, competitor pricing, technical documentation, and contrary evidence.

3.9.1 Intent- and field-specific source recipes

Discovery must select source classes from the deliverable contract, not from one global domain list. For an opportunity-discovery plus implementation report, generate different query families for different evidence cells:

- pain and demand signals: user forums, issue trackers, support communities, reviews, procurement requests, search trends, and credible surveys;
- competitor reality: official product pages, pricing, changelogs, documentation, app marketplaces, customer reviews, shutdown records, and funding/company filings where relevant;
- build feasibility: official API documentation, SDK repositories, platform limits, terms of service, security requirements, hosting/database documentation, and maintained implementation examples;
- market or regulatory constraints: primary statutes, regulator guidance, standards bodies, official statistics, and high-quality industry research;
- acquisition feasibility: channel rules, marketplace policies, ad costs where available, community norms, and comparable launch evidence.

GitHub, Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, and Reddit can be valuable within those recipes, but their role must be labeled. A GitHub issue may be strong direct evidence of a technical defect; a Reddit complaint may be a useful demand signal; neither alone proves market size or factual consensus.

Query generation should include the artifact field, target user, domain vocabulary, geography/time window when relevant, and desired source class. Search should pivot from macro-market terms to narrow workflow and failure-language terms such as "manual," "spreadsheet," "workaround," "API missing," "feature request," "pricing," "integration," and "alternative" when opportunity gaps are being tested.

3.12 PolicyOne is currently universal rather than a subset methodology

Files:

- ResearchOne PolicyOne
- backend/src/constants/prompts.ts
- backend/src/services/openrouter/openrouterService.ts
- backend/src/constants/modeOverlays.ts

SYSTEM_PROMPTS are built with withPreamble(), so the PolicyOne reasoning-first preamble affects general roles broadly. The test also ran with GENERAL_EPISTEMIC_RESEARCH, whose overlay forces competing hypotheses even though the confirmed intent was opportunity discovery.

This conflates at least six independent concepts:

- what the user wants produced;
- how deep and expensive the research should be;
- whether the user's premise should be accepted, qualified, verified, or challenged;
- how aggressively competing viewpoints should be sought;
- which evidence classes are appropriate;
- whether PolicyOne's investigative method should be applied.

Required correction:

3.12.1 Always-on Evidence Integrity Core

Every research mode must retain a small, non-adversarial integrity core:

- no fabricated facts, quotations, calculations, products, or citations;
- traceable provenance for material claims;
- distinction among sourced fact, calculation, inference, hypothesis, and recommendation;
- source-quality, recency, independence, conflict-of-interest, and duplication assessment;
- uncertainty calibrated to the actual evidence;
- citation-to-claim entailment checks;
- corrections when the available evidence materially contradicts a factual assertion.

This is how ResearchOne minimizes bias and misinformation across all research types. It must not force every report to debate the user's framing or create artificial counterclaims.

3.12.2 First-class AnalysisMode axis

1. neutral_synthesis
   Pure research from the best available sources. Answer the stated question, summarize uncertainty and limitations, and do not manufacture a controversy. Default for reference research, descriptive factual work, ordinary literature reviews, how-to, feasibility, implementation, and many opportunity-discovery tasks.

2. bias_aware_synthesis
   Broadens source ownership, geography, incentives, schools of thought, and supporting/contrary evidence to reduce selection and framing bias. It still answers the user's question and does not become a debunking exercise.

3. claim_verification
   Used when the requested artifact is a truth assessment, fact-check, misinformation analysis, story verification, or adjudication of a specific claim. Requires explicit claim decomposition, evidence for and against, verdict criteria, and unresolved uncertainty.

4. policyone_investigative
   Available for investigations, anomaly correlation, contested-history work, institutional-conflict analysis, evidence-suppression hypotheses, or explicit user selection. It preserves contradictions and anomalies without treating them as automatically true.

5. hybrid_dual_lane
   Runs a conventional evidence lane and a PolicyOne anomaly-preservation lane, then compares them without forcing either lane to win. Appropriate for selected high-stakes, contested, or institutionally sensitive questions.

Depth remains a separate quick | standard | deep | exhaustive axis. "Deep research" does not mean "PolicyOne," "skeptical," or "adversarial."

3.12.3 Premise treatment

Premise handling must also be explicit:

- accept_as_scope: treat the wording as task context without adjudicating it;
- note_uncertainty: preserve the task while replacing an absolute with a calibrated caveat;
- verify: make the premise itself a research question;
- adversarial_test: actively seek falsifiers because the user or research design requires it.

For the failed request, "no fail" should map to note_uncertainty. The report should say that no outcome can be guaranteed, translate the phrase into transparent risk-adjusted success criteria, and then deliver all ten opportunities. A phrase such as "guaranteed," "best," "true," or "proven" must never by itself select PolicyOne or convert the speech act.

PolicyOne activation rules:

- Explicit user selection controls unless applying it would conflict with a higher-priority safety or legal requirement.
- A saved profile may select it, but the plan preview must show it.
- The classifier may recommend it, but the user-confirmed brief is authoritative.
- Do not auto-activate it merely because the request is "deep," unusual, non-consensus, or emphatically worded.
- Do not auto-activate it for ordinary market opportunity, implementation, comparison, how-to, direct lookup, or descriptive research.
- Record analysis mode, premise treatment, methodology modules, and rationale in plan, run, report metadata, and UI.

PolicyOne quality safeguards:

- Preserve source wording and anomalous claims literally.
- Do not treat consensus as proof.
- Do not treat non-consensus as proof.
- Do not convert absence of evidence into evidence of suppression.
- Test ordinary explanations, measurement error, selection bias, fraud, and suppression hypotheses symmetrically.
- Require provenance and independent corroboration for every lane.
- Clearly label the PolicyOne lane as a methodology, not as a higher truth tier.
- Preserve contradictions through synthesis rather than manufacturing them.

Do not delete PolicyOne's differentiating features. Scope and discipline them.

4. TARGET RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

4.1 Keep the controlling axes independent

Every confirmed run must persist these independent decisions:

- Intent: what must be produced.
- Secondary intents: additional deliverables or analyses.
- Depth: how broad and iterative the work should be.
- Analysis mode: neutral synthesis, bias-aware synthesis, claim verification, PolicyOne investigative, or hybrid dual lane.
- Premise treatment: accept as scope, note uncertainty, verify, or adversarially test.
- Methodology modules: always-on evidence integrity plus any explicitly selected systematic, quantitative, PolicyOne, or domain-specific module.
- Epistemic posture: descriptive, balanced, skeptical, or adversarial, subordinate to the confirmed analysis mode.
- Source strategy: which evidence classes, freshness, domains, and minimum coverage are required.
- Deliverable contract: exact artifacts, counts, fields, formats, and success criteria.

Do not let researchObjective or engineVersion overwrite intent.

4.2 Contract-first execution flow

Use this pipeline:

1. Intake and clarification.
2. Compile and validate ResearchBriefV2.
3. User confirms the brief and assumptions.
4. Create an immutable DeliverableContract.
5. Build a coverage matrix from required artifact fields and subquestions.
6. Generate query families by field and source class.
7. Search, ingest, assess, and deduplicate sources.
8. Run gap analysis.
9. Pivot and repeat within the depth/budget limit until coverage or saturation criteria are met.
10. Build a structured EvidenceGraph.
11. Run only the selected specialists once.
12. Create the structured artifact first.
13. Validate exact counts, required fields, constraints, and evidence links deterministically.
14. Render narrative sections from the validated artifact.
15. Run citation entailment, quantitative audit, epistemic verifier, and contract auditor.
16. Repair failed criteria in a bounded loop.
17. Generate plain-language output only from a passing primary report.
18. Finalize with a truthful status and complete citations.

4.3 Coverage matrix

For an opportunity report, rows should be opportunity candidates and columns should include:

- target customer;
- painful problem;
- demand evidence;
- competitor evidence;
- current workaround;
- differentiation;
- 24-hour MVP scope;
- required stack/services;
- monetization;
- acquisition route;
- risks/disqualifiers;
- cheapest validation test;
- confidence;
- source IDs.

Do not draft the final ten until candidate coverage supports selection. Keep more than ten candidates during research, score them, then select the top ten.

4.4 Opportunity scoring

Use an explicit, inspectable scoring model. Example dimensions:

- pain urgency: 15%;
- evidence of demand: 15%;
- competitive whitespace: 15%;
- 24-hour MVP feasibility: 20%;
- founder/tool fit: 10%;
- monetization speed: 10%;
- customer acquisition accessibility: 10%;
- regulatory/security burden: negative 5% or a disqualifier.

Weights must be visible and adjustable. Scores must reference evidence and assumptions. Avoid fake precision; use ranges or ordinal bands when data is weak.

4.5 Structured artifact before prose

For the failing test, synthesis should first produce a validated object resembling:

- analysisMode, premiseTreatment, and methodologyModules;
- scoringModel;
- opportunities[10];
  - rank;
  - name;
  - targetCustomer;
  - problem;
  - evidenceOfGap[];
  - existingAlternatives[];
  - differentiation;
  - mvp24HourScope;
  - excludedFromMvp[];
  - stackAndServices[];
  - monetization;
  - acquisitionPlan;
  - risks[];
  - validationExperiment;
  - successProbabilityBand;
  - evidenceIds[];
  - buildPrompt;
  - testPrompt;
  - deploymentPrompt;
  - acceptanceCriteria[];
- portfolioComparison;
- recommendedFirstBuild;
- sources[];

Validate this object with a schema. Then render the report. Narrative prose must never be the only representation of a requested enumerated artifact.

4.6 Capability-based specialist selection and EZ/Lab boundaries

Do not choose specialists because a run is labeled "research," "deep," "EZ," or "Lab." Compile the smallest capability plan that can satisfy the contract and coverage matrix.

For the failed request, market-gap assessment, feasibility, and implementation planning are relevant; a generic adversarial epistemic swarm is not. Candidate generation can be broad, but expensive specialists should run only after preliminary candidates exist and only on the candidates or fields that need their capability.

EZ Research should be a lower-depth preset with a smaller source and specialist budget, while retaining the same contract, provenance, citation, and validation guarantees. Lab Mode should describe an execution/build workspace, not silently disable research when the task includes evidence-dependent claims. Deep Research should expand coverage and iteration, not automatically change the analysis mode.

Persist a rationale for every specialist invocation. Enforce one invocation per planned specialist per attempt, explicit retry records, per-stage token/time budgets, and a visible degraded state when a required capability fails. Cache reusable source assessments and candidate evidence so repair loops do not repeat discovery wholesale.


5. WORLD-CLASS RESEARCH QUALITY CONTROLS

5.1 Source assessment

Store separate scores for:

- provenance;
- relevance;
- recency;
- methodological quality;
- independence;
- directness of support;
- conflict of interest;
- extractability;
- corroboration;
- contradiction role.

Do not collapse these into the existing evidence tier. Evidence tier describes the claim/evidence relationship; source assessment describes source characteristics.

5.2 Evidence records

Every evidence record should include:

- evidence ID;
- source ID;
- canonical URL;
- title;
- author or organization;
- published/updated date;
- retrieval date;
- exact passage or data values;
- locator;
- supported or challenged claim IDs;
- extraction method;
- source class;
- provenance score;
- limitations;
- independence group.

5.3 Citation integrity

A report passes only when:

- every material factual claim has a usable citation;
- the cited passage entails the claim;
- sources are present and accessible or clearly archived;
- quotations are exact;
- numerical claims identify their inputs;
- references render complete bibliographic data or a complete web citation;
- internal chunk IDs remain available for audit but are not the sole reader citation.

5.4 Honest completion states

Use these report states:

- drafting;
- needs_more_research;
- needs_revision;
- verification_failed;
- contract_failed;
- completed_degraded;
- completed;
- finalized.

Finalized must mean every required gate passed. It must not mean merely that the pipeline reached the save function.

5.5 Deep-research stop rules

Stop searching when one of these is true:

- all critical coverage cells meet their evidence threshold;
- new discovery rounds produce negligible unique evidence and saturation is documented;
- the confirmed cost/time budget is exhausted;
- required sources are inaccessible and the limitation is explicit;
- the user cancels.

Do not stop merely because two discovery rounds ran or five queries completed.
